Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what data glor1eux ("we") collects and how it is used.

1. Data we collect

  • TikTok account info (via the user.info.basic scope): your open ID, display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, stored securely on our server and used only to upload or publish videos you choose.
  • Videos and captions you prepare in the Service, kept only as long as needed to publish them.

2. How we use data

We use this data only to provide the Service: showing your connected account and uploading or publishing videos when you click publish or send to drafts. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Retention and deletion

When you disconnect your account, we delete your tokens and account info. You can request deletion of any data by emailing us. You can also revoke access from your TikTok app settings.

4. Security

Tokens and credentials are kept server-side and never exposed in the browser or in public code.
